Nine apps for the Miyoo Mini Plus (they need its Wi-Fi). They share one
interface with 16 themes, and keep logins and 2FA seeds on the card only
encrypted. Each can be installed on its own from its releases page.

- [**MiniScale**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniScale): an unofficial Tailscale client. QR-code login, exit nodes, Taildrop, connects at boot.
- [**MiniTape**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniTape): music from Navidrome or any Subsonic server; keeps playing in the Onion menu and other apps.
- [**MiniRack**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniRack): CPU, memory, disks and temperatures of your servers, from Glances or Beszel.
- [**MiniKey**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniKey): 2FA (TOTP) codes behind a button-combination unlock code.
- [**MiniTop**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniTop): running programs, boot scripts, and a clock sync that works over HTTPS.
- [**MiniMart**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniMart): themes, icon packs, apps and homebrew games, each checked against its SHA-256.
- [**MiniBook**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniBook): this documentation, readable offline on the device.
- [**MiniRadar**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniRadar): the devices on your network, and Wake-on-LAN.
- [**MiniPulse**](https://github.com/zakijss/MiniPulse): a speed test against Cloudflare, LibreSpeed or OpenSpeedTest.

Tested on a Miyoo Mini Plus with Onion 4.4. The original Mini (no Wi-Fi) is
not supported.
