# retroshelf

<sup>by retroshelf</sup>

[**retroshelf - official page**](https://www.retroshelf.org/help/plugins/onionos)

Automatically sync play sessions from a Miyoo Mini Plus running OnionOS to your retroshelf profile. Sessions played offline are queued and synced the next time WiFi is available.

[Download the OnionOS plugin](https://www.retroshelf.org/downloads/onionos)
